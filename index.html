Sources & methodology

The receipts: where these numbers actually come from
Biomarker reference ranges
Fasting glucose, HbA1c, lipids, and blood pressure ranges are informed by CDC, American Heart Association, and NCEP ATP III clinical guidelines.
Specialist markers
ApoB, Lp(a), homocysteine, Vitamin D, and fasting insulin ranges reflect commonly-cited values from cardiology and endocrinology literature. Unlike glucose or blood pressure, there isn't one single official body setting these cutoffs, so treat these as a reasonable reference point, not a clinical diagnosis line.
Estimated biological age
A simplified estimate based on how your biomarker score compares to a neutral baseline, adjusted against your chronological age. This is not a clinical epigenetic clock and does not implement the Levine PhenoAge algorithm or any specific published aging clock — it's a proxy meant to give directional feedback, not a lab-grade measurement.
Calorie and macro targets
Calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for basal metabolic rate, standard activity-level multipliers, and Acceptable Macronutrient Distribution Ranges (AMDR) from the USDA Dietary Guidelines for Americans.
Disease risk context
Reflects general, well-established associations from the epidemiological literature (e.g. chronic inflammation and cancer risk, insulin resistance and dementia risk, homocysteine and cognitive decline, midlife hypertension and dementia risk). These are broad research associations, not a diagnosis, a risk score, or a citation to one specific study.
Nutrition data
Food search and macro lookups use the USDA FoodData Central API.
Professional directories
Referral suggestions link to Quest Diagnostics, Labcorp, Walk-In Lab, Galleri/GRAIL, the National Society of Genetic Counselors, C2N Diagnostics (PrecivityAD), EatRight.org (Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics), ACE Fitness, the American Academy of Sleep Medicine, Psychology Today, and CBDCE.
Biomarq — reference ranges informed by CDC, AHA, and ATP III clinical guidelines. Not medical advice.
